Dose in

Put your dosing cup on the scale, tare, then add beans. Weighing the beans rather than trusting the grinder's timer is what makes dose a measurement instead of an assumption.

Grounds out

Tare with the empty portafilter on the scale, grind into it, and weigh again. The difference from the dose is retention — grounds still inside the grinder — and it is the reason a "single dose" workflow rarely gives you back what you put in.

Armed, the scale auto-tares when you set the cup down and the timer starts on first flow. If it guesses wrong, Start now overrides it — the machine is a convenience, not a cage.
