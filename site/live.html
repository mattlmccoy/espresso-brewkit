Stream weight from a Bluetooth scale and capture the shot as it pours.

01 — Connect

Chrome, Edge and Opera only — Firefox and Safari have no Web Bluetooth, and on iOS no browser does. The demo needs no hardware and exercises the whole pipeline, so you can see what a shot looks like before you own a supported scale.

Scale not in the list? A BLE device stops advertising while something else is connected to it — so if nRF Connect, the vendor app, or another browser tab still holds the connection, it becomes invisible here. Disconnect there first. Failing that, power-cycle the scale: many sleep and stop advertising after a minute or two idle.

A device with no services is normal, not broken. Web Bluetooth only exposes services the page asked for in advance — there is no way to list them all. Wide scan covers the ranges cheap modules use. If it still finds nothing, read the real UUID off the scale with nRF Connect (free, iOS and Android) and paste it above.

02 — Teach it your scale

Undocumented scales send an unlabelled frame. Put known masses on and brewkit works out where the weight lives — put something on, type what your scale reads, capture. Three captures at different masses is enough; include zero.
