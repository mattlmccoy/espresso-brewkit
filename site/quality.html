Per-shot scores

Three methods, because they fail differently. The plain z-score is computed from a mean and standard deviation that the outlier itself inflates — with a small sample, one extreme point can push the threshold out past itself and escape detection. The modified z-score uses the median and MAD, which an outlier cannot drag. Where they disagree, believe the modified score.
