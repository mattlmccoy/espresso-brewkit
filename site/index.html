Measurement tools for espresso.

A browser-based toolkit for logging shots, propagating measurement uncertainty, and finding which variables actually move extraction yield — plus the design for an open-source scale that records all of it without a phone.

Tools

🧮

Calculator

Brix to TDS to extraction yield, brew ratio, and average flow. Solve for whichever value you are missing.

 📓

Shot log

Record shots, derive everything that follows, and import or export a single CSV. Reads the old one-file-per-shot format too.

 📈

Explore

Regress yield against any variable, fit a plane against two, and read the correlation matrix. With confidence bands, not just a trend line.

 🔍

Quality

Outlier detection by three methods, and the descriptive statistics that tell you whether your prep is repeatable enough to model.

 🔬

Uncertainty

GUM propagation through the yield equation, with a variance budget that shows which measurement is actually limiting you.

 🛠

Scale design

Hardware, firmware, and data-model specification for a low-profile scale that logs shots on-device and drives a detachable display.

How it works

Shots live in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and the export is a plain CSV you own.

The same CSV schema is what the scale writes to its own flash, so the hardware and these tools share one format.
