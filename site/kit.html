Add a bag

Freezing pauses staling rather than stopping it, so age accrues before the freezer and after it, and barely during. A bag frozen on day 5 and opened in June is a five-day-old coffee, not a five-month-old one — and dating it that way is the difference between a useful roast-age column and a misleading one. Freeze portions, not the bag: a portion goes in once and comes out once, because opening frozen beans condenses water onto them and putting them back locks it in.
