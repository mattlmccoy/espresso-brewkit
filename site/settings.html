Settings

Everything the app lets you change, in one place. Nothing here needs a scale plugged in, and nothing here touches your shots.

Theme

Four of them. Light and dark follow the system until you pick one; the other two never do. Terminal is green phosphor on black, and Glass translucent panes over a coloured ground. The dial is the same instrument in every one of them — a theme changes the material, not the shape.

When the app takes a reading

These decide when a number on the scale stops being a wobble and becomes a measurement. If the session grabs a weight you were still adding to, raise the hold; if it sits waiting while you stand there, lower the settle. Every scale is different, and the defaults are a guess at yours. Export a session's readings on the Live page first — the file shows what your scale actually does, which beats guessing twice.

What a shot starts at

Leave these empty and the brew method decides — 18 g at 1:2 for espresso, 22 g at 1:16 for a pour over. Fill them in and every new shot starts there instead.

Refractometry

Extraction yield is worked out from a Brix reading using this factor, for every shot as it is saved. It is the one number behind every yield in the log, so changing it changes what all of them are said to have extracted.

Sound and taps

What the app does without being looked at: the noises it makes, and the taps it listens for.

Sound cues

Tones and a buzz on the beats that matter: a tare, a dose landing in its window, the countdown, the stop. The phone viewer keeps its own separate setting, on the phone.

Tap gestures

Tapping the scale platter drives the app: two taps to begin or undo, three to tare, two then hold to change brew method.

Tap sensitivity

How many grams of platter deflection counts as a tap.

Learning your taps needs the scale in front of you, so the tuner itself stays on Live, under Device settings.

Drip lag, per machine

How long each machine keeps dripping after the pump stops. The app measures this from your own shots and uses it to call the stop early, so the cup lands on target instead of past it. Under three shots there is not enough to go on and it assumes one second.

Finding a scale

Only for a scale the chooser will not show. Most scales need neither of these, and both are remembered, so a scale that needs one needs it once.

For a scale nothing else finds. Leave empty unless you have one.

Explanations you have hidden

Every explanation in the app can be dismissed for good. This brings them all back, wherever you hid them.

What is stored on this device

All of it, in this browser, on this machine. Nothing is sent anywhere. Taking a copy, or moving it to another computer, is on the Backup page.

Backup and restore

Resetting settings leaves your shots, bags, grinders and machines alone. It only puts the choices on this page back.
