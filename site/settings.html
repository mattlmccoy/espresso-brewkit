Settings

Everything the app lets you change, in one place. Some of it was only reachable with a scale connected and a collapsed panel expanded; some of it was a constant in the source with no way to reach it at all.

Theme

Six of them. Light and dark follow the system until you pick one; the other four never do. Terminal is green phosphor on black, Machined a lit instrument, Glass translucent panes over a coloured ground, and Bloom a soft plum and honey. The dial is the same instrument in every one of them — a theme changes the material, not the shape.

When the app takes a reading

These decide when a number on the scale becomes a measurement the session acts on. They were constructor options that nothing ever passed, so every shot ever pulled used the values below — and they were picked by reasoning about what a scale probably does, not by watching one. If the flow captures things it should not, or sits waiting when it should move on, this is where to correct it. Export a session's readings on the Live page first: the file shows what your scale actually does, which is the only honest way to set these.

What a shot starts at

Leave these empty and the brew method decides — 18 g at 1:2 for espresso, 22 g at 1:16 for a pour over. Fill them in and every new shot starts there instead.

Refractometry

One number, and it has been quietly governing the whole log: extraction yield is derived from Brix for every shot as it is saved, using this factor. It could be read by the store and written by nothing — the calculator let you change it for one calculation and then threw the change away.

Sound and taps

Both were buttons on the Live page, and only appeared once a scale was connected.

Sound cues

Tones and a buzz on the beats that matter: a tare, a dose landing in its window, the countdown, the stop. The phone viewer keeps its own separate setting, on the phone.

Tap gestures

Tapping the scale platter drives the app: two taps to begin or undo, three to tare, two then hold to change brew method.

Tap sensitivity

How many grams of platter deflection counts as a tap.

Learning your taps needs the scale in front of you, so the tuner itself stays on Live, under Device settings.

Drip lag, per machine

How long each machine keeps dripping after the pump stops. It is measured from your own shots and used to call the stop early, and until now there was no way to see it, correct it, or start it over. Under three shots the app does not trust it yet and uses one second.

Finding a scale

These were real options on the Live page that reset every time the page loaded, so a scale that needed them needed them typed in again on every visit.

For a scale nothing else finds. Leave empty unless you have one.

Explanations you have hidden

Every explanation in the app can be dismissed for good. This brings them all back — a preference you cannot reverse is a trap, and until now the one control that reversed it was in the Live page footer, including for notes hidden on other pages.

What is stored on this device

All of it, in this browser, on this machine. Nothing is sent anywhere. Taking a copy, or moving it to another computer, is on the Backup page.

Backup and restore

Resetting settings leaves your shots, bags, grinders and machines alone. It only puts the choices on this page back.
